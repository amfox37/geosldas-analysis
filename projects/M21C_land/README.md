# M21C_land

Diagnostics for the M21C land DA configuration on the **cubed-sphere CF0360** tiling
(about 1.19 M land tiles), from the `M21C_testing` experiments on Discover
(`/gpfsm/dnb06/projects/p284/M21C_testing` = `/discover/nobackup/projects/land_da/M21C_testing`).

This is separate from `M21C_ls`, which covers the M36 Land Sweeper manuscript.

## Environment

Runs on Discover, where the experiment output lives. Use the GEOSpyD Python 3.13 environment:

```
/usr/local/other/GEOSpyD/25.3.1-0/2025-10-07/envs/py3.13/bin/python3
```

It has netCDF4, pandas, scipy, cartopy and jupyter, and needs no `g5_modules`.

## Notebooks

- `notebooks/cf0360_grid_and_tiles.ipynb`
  - Explains the three spatial layers: cubed-sphere grid cells (6 x 360 x 360), Pfafstetter catchments, and
    tiles (cell x catchment). Figures come from the BCS 30-arcsec rasters.
  - Shows how obs are assigned to tiles, and checks the rule against ObsFcstAna `tilenum`. For a cube-sphere
    tile space, GEOSldas uses a lat/lon "pert grid" (4N x 3N; `DE1440xPE1080` for C360). It picks the tile
    whose centre of mass is nearest (L1 distance in degrees) among the tiles whose centres fall in the obs's
    pert-grid cell. The obs is therefore not always inside its tile.
- `notebooks/cf0360_allsp_may2019_ofa_maps.ipynb`
  - ObsFcstAna maps and daily time series for the May 2019 all-species DA run
    (`M21C_test_CF0360_allsp_superob025ml_OL6sc_xc03125`) vs the 6-member open loop
    (`M21C_test_CF0360_OL6_superob025ml_clim`).
  - Each sensor is mapped on **its own obs grid**, so every coloured cell is one real obs footprint and grey
    gaps are real: EASE2 M36 for SMOS/SMAP/CYGNSS, 0.25 deg for H SAF super-obs, tile space for MODIS SCF.
    Robinson projection, north of 60S.
  - Obs are matched between runs, and the DA run's scaled obs are used as O for both (the OL ran with
    `scale = .false.`).
  - **Temporary:** uses `NMIN = 10` obs per cell instead of the repo default of 20, because one month is
    too short for 20. The override is specific to this notebook.
  - Caches go to `M21C_testing/analysis_M21C_land/`, outside the repo. Figures go to `figures/`
    (`*.png` is git-ignored).

- `notebooks/cf0360_allsp_may2019_states.ipynb`
  - Model states SFMC and RZMC for the same two runs. allsp vs OL6 uses the daily 0.5 deg `tavg24_2d_lnd_Nx` files,
    because OL6 wrote nothing else: daily global and latitude-band means, and maps of the monthly-mean and
    31 May differences.
  - allsp only: analysis increments (ANA - FCST from `inst3_1d_lndfcstana_Nt`) summed over the month in tile
    space, averaged to cube cells with `frac_cell` weights, and drawn on the true cube-cell shapes.

- `notebooks/cf0360_allsp_may2019_suspicious_increments.ipynb`
  - Flags excessive analysis increments per land tile and cycle, and maps the worst tile per cube cell.
    Groups: A = large compared with the forecast ensemble spread (z > 3 plus a minimum size; TSURF flags that come
    with a snow update are kept separate); B = physically large (|dW| > 100 mm, |dRZMC| > 0.05, |dSFMC| > 0.1,
    TC > 5 K); C = root zone pinned at saturation with collapsed spread for 5+ days after DA wetting;
    D = assimilated obs with |normalized innovation| > 5. Includes a top-events table with the obs assimilated nearby.

- `notebooks/cf0360_allsp_thin25km_3mo_{ofa_maps,states,suspicious_increments}.ipynb`
  - The three notebooks above, copied for 1 May to 31 July 2019 and the all-species run with H SAF thinned to
    25 km (`M21C_test_CF0360_allsp_thin25km_1f0e9ef_xc03125`, GEOSldas commit 1f0e9ef), again against OL6.
  - `NMIN = 20` (repo default). Cycle counts and colour scales follow the period. SMAP has no obs from 20 June
    to 22 July 2019 in this run. The OFA maps notebook needs roughly three times the memory of the May version.

- `notebooks/cf0360_hsaf_superob_fov.ipynb`
  - H SAF ASCAT raw obs vs 0.25 deg super-obs for one cycle (2019-05-01 03z) over a small box in SW Kansas,
    comparing `M21C_test_CF0360_baseline_HSAF_xc03125` with `M21C_test_CF0360_superob025ml_OL6sc_HSAF_xc03125`.
  - Rebuilds the reader's QC, the aggregation into tiles or 0.25 deg cells, and the `get_obs_pred` field of view
    (25 km Gaussian vs 0.141 deg uniform) in Python. Each step is checked against the `ldas_log` and ObsFcstAna
    (positions, tile numbers and `fcst`, all exact).
  - Shows footprint size, footprint overlap between neighbouring obs, obs spacing and the resulting conditioning of R.
  - Generated by `M21C_testing/analysis_superob_fov/make_superob_fov_notebook.py` (outside the repo).

- `notebooks/hsaf_obs_thinning_explained.ipynb`
  - How the H SAF ASCAT obs thinning was chosen, for reviewers of the GEOSldas change. The original proposal was
    `obs_param_nml(56:58)%thin_ratio` (Fibonacci-lattice binning, `fibgrid_thinning.F90`, GEOSldas_GridComp commit
    `ca872c7`, never pushed). It was replaced by `thin_dist_km` (greedy minimum distance, subroutine `thin_min_dist`
    in `clsm_ensupd_read_obs.F90`, commit `a499c55`). Sections: the grid; why index thinning fails; why binning
    cannot separate obs at 1-in-2 (hexagonal sub-lattices); greedy thinning; caveats (visiting order, per window,
    between satellites, rounding at lattice distances); what reaches the CF0360 tiles; the Fortran of both methods
    compiled and checked against the Python versions (`thin_min_dist` is extracted from the reader for this).
  - Set `THIN_RATIO` / `THIN_DIST_KM` in the settings cell to try other namelist values.
  - **Runs locally** (~2 min), not on Discover: `regrid` env, local H121 files (Jan 2020), the CF0360 tilecoord from
    `GEOSldas_diagnostics/test_data`, and `gfortran` + the local GEOSldas checkout for §9.
  - H SAF `location_id` = `i mod (2N+1)` (not `i + N`); file latitudes are geodetic.

- `notebooks/cf0360_hsaf_superob_fov_v2.ipynb`
  - Local companion of `cf0360_hsaf_superob_fov.ipynb`: the new H SAF super-ob field of view (GEOSldas_GridComp
    `feature/amfox/hsaf-superobs`, commit `9624356`). The old footprint (`e9c365d`) was a uniform 0.141° disk; the new one
    is a Gaussian with C = FOV²·I + S, where S is the spread of the super-ob's raw obs. Both are compared against the
    exact target, the mean of the raw obs' 12.5 km footprints: one-cell schematic, spread S and σ_eff, four-footprint
    maps, region statistics, partial cells, super-obs lost by the 12.5 km assignment tolerance, predicted values,
    and footprint overlap.
  - **Runs locally** (~45 s, `regrid` env), needs no Discover data: local H121 (Jan 2020; same SW Kansas box, 03z
    ascending pass), the CF0360 tilecoord from `GEOSldas_diagnostics/test_data`, a window of the BCS 30" tile raster
    around the Kansas box (`~/Desktop/M21C_land_local/superob_fov/tile_raster_kansas.npz`, cut once on Discover) for true
    tile shapes (cube-cell edges are derived from the tilecoord; outside the window, tiles are drawn as centres of mass), and
    SMAP L4 `SFMC_FCST` (M09, 2019-11-01) mapped to CF0360 tiles as the field for predicted values. The ObsFcstAna
    checks are in the Discover notebook only.

- `notebooks/cf0360_superob_vs_thinning_obsspace.ipynb`
  - H SAF on CF0360, in obs space: raw mode vs 0.25° super-obs (new Gaussian footprint) vs minimum-distance thinning
    (20 and 25 km), all built from the same raw H121 obs and predicted from the same background, for the 7 windows of
    2019-05-01 (03z–21z), Metop-A/B/C. Counts and spacing, maps over SW Kansas, O − F statistics (including by number
    of raw obs per super-ob), and the spatial correlation of O − F.
  - Checks against the baseline run's ObsFcstAna: raw-mode predictions reproduce `fcst`, and the per-tile z-score obs
    scaling, recovered as `b = √obsvar / errstd`, `a = obs − b · raw`, reproduces the scaled obs exactly.
  - Data fetched once from Discover into `~/Desktop/M21C_land_local/cf0360_obs_fcst_20190501/` (raw H121 files,
    `SFMC_FCST` per window from `M21C_test_CF0360_baseline_HSAF_xc03125`, its ObsFcstAna). Runs locally in ~75 s.
  - Obs space only, against one background: it does not show the impact on analyses or forecasts (needs cycling runs).

- `notebooks/cf0360_cycling_superob_vs_thinning.ipynb`
  - Examples from the H SAF-only cycling pair `M21C_test_CF0360_sob025fp_HSAF_xc03125` (0.25° super-obs, `9624356`) and
    `M21C_test_CF0360_thin25km_HSAF_xc03125` (`thin_dist_km = 25`, `5e220ff`), with `M21C_test_CF0360_OL_monitor_HSAF`
    (H SAF monitored in raw mode) as the reference, on 2019-05-01 (shared first forecast) and 2019-05-10. Shows over SW
    Kansas the obs each run assimilated, their footprints, tile weights (one super-ob vs the thinned obs around it;
    weight per unit area), and SFMC increments; globally, counts, spacing and increment statistics, and each run's
    forecast against the raw H SAF obs and against the monitored SMAP/SMOS Tb (same obs in all runs).
  - Rebuilds every run's obs offline and checks them against its ObsFcstAna (positions exact, `fcst` to ~1e-4).
    The super-ob run uses the scaling derived for the old super-obs (deliberate; the thinning run's raw-obs scaling is
    correct for it): for the new super-obs this adds ~0.015 m³/m³ of tile-to-tile noise, which confounds the comparison.
  - Includes the rerun `M21C_test_CF0360_sob025fp_OL1sc_HSAF_xc03125` (same super-obs, raw-obs/OL scaling; data in
    `~/Desktop/M21C_land_local/cf0360_cycling_sob025fp_OL1sc/`). With consistent scaling, super-obs and thinning are
    indistinguishable after 10 days (fit to raw H SAF obs and to SMAP/SMOS Tb); the old run's larger increments, Kansas
    overshoot (section 6.1) and worse Tb fit were the scaling. Super-obs give somewhat smaller increments for the same fit.
  - Section 5.3: where the obs fall pass after pass. Thinning keeps a different set of grid points each pass (even for
    identical raw obs), so tile weights vary ~17% between passes; super-obs sit at the same position every pass.
  - Data fetched once with `scripts/fetch_cycling_superob_vs_thin.py` (run on Discover with GEOSpyD) into
    `~/Desktop/M21C_land_local/cf0360_cycling_sob_vs_thin/`; 5/1 raw H121 comes from `cf0360_obs_fcst_20190501/`.
    Runs locally in ~3.5 min. ANA − FCST has a ~1e-3 noise floor (nonzero even in the OL), so increments are counted above it.
