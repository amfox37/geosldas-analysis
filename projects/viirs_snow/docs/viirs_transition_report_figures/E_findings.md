# Analysis E: findings and recommendation (numbers in E_summary.md)

**Question:** after the 2023-08-01 switch, is there a spin-up period in C1 (Terra vs J2) and C2 (T+A vs J1+J2) that should be excluded from the contrasts?

1. **There is almost no snow to spin up in the first 8 weeks.** Aug+Sep is about 1.1-1.2% of the snow-year integral of NH monthly-mean SWE in every arm. NH snow tiles (SWE > 1 kg m-2) go from about 1100 in week 0 to about 9700 in week 8 and about 38,000 by week 19 (`E_1`). Excluding 4-8 weeks would remove about 1% of the SWE integral, but 10-14% of NH absolute increments (new snow and melt-out edges are updated at the season's start).
2. **No step and no slow adjustment in the increments.** The VIIRS-arm / MODIS-control ratio of NH weekly absolute increments is the same in weeks 0-19 of 2023 as in 2024 and 2025 (T about 0.6-0.9, TA about 0.5-0.67; `E_5`), already in week 0. The reduction relative to MODIS is a permanent property of the configuration (analysis B), not a transient. First-8-week totals in 2023 are within 5% of the mean of 2024-25 in every arm (ratio 0.95-1.02).
3. **J1/J2 obs counts and O-F are in line with later years.** Weekly NH counts and rms O-F in 2023 are within the 2024-25 range from week 0 on (`E_4`); no sign of a sensor spin-up. One exception: J1 and J2 counts in week 8 of 2023 (26 Sep - 2 Oct) are about 25-30% below 2024-25 (11.4k vs 16k J1). Not investigated (possibly missing source days).
4. **The C1/C2 differences grow from zero as snow appears, not because of a spin-up.** The SWE rms difference between the MODIS control and the VIIRS arm (identical start state) rises from 0.16 (C1) / 0.20 (C2) kg m-2 in week 0 to about 1 kg m-2 at week 8 and 2.0-2.4 at week 19 (`E_2`). Relative to the DA effect (control - OL) it is stable at about 0.5 (C1) and 0.45 (C2) from week 4 on. The same calendar weeks in 2024 and 2025 follow the same curve from week 4 (the difference already includes the previous year's divergence, so weeks 0-3 are higher there). SH rms differences peak at 0.7-1.3 kg m-2 in weeks 7-9 and decay as SH snow melts out.
5. **The only transient in the first weeks is the divergence itself:** C1/C2 start at exactly 0 at the shared restart (by construction), so any metric that averages over a window starting on 2023-08-01 slightly understates the contrast. NH ratio to the DA effect is 0.2 in weeks 0-2, 0.4-0.6 from week 3.

**Recommendation:** no exclusion window is needed for the snow-year contrasts. The VIIRS switch has no detectable spin-up in increments, innovations or SWE/SCF beyond the natural growth of the difference from the shared restart, and the first 8 weeks hold about 1% of the SWE integral. Keep 2023-08-01 itself excluded (as in analyses B/D, it has only 3-hourly files and a window-boundary day). If a conservative choice is wanted, mask the first 4 weeks (1-28 Aug) for C1/C2: it costs less than 0.5% of the SWE integral and removes the weeks in which the contrast is still near its zero start.

**Caveats:** single member, one transition (2023); the 2024-25 comparison windows are not same-start, and 2023's week-8 obs dip is unexplained. Skill (analysis C) was not re-scored with a mask; SNOTEL/GHCN station snow in Aug-Sep is minimal, so the effect on station metrics should also be tiny (not tested). The NH tile count uses SWE > 1 kg m-2 on the daily mean.
